Your profile reads like a resume. Not an offer.

Most attorneys fill LinkedIn with degrees and bar admissions. That does not convert. A potential client is not looking for a CV: they are looking for someone who understands their problem and can solve it.

2 / 6
Step 1

Optimize your profile for clients, not for judges.

Headline: "I help businesses avoid costly employment lawsuits" instead of "Attorney specializing in employment law". Summary: start with the problem you solve, not your career timeline.

3 / 6
Step 2

Post content that builds trust.

3 formats that work: analysis of recent rulings in plain language, opinions on legal news affecting your niche, and anonymized success stories with concrete outcomes. Twice a week is enough to stay visible.

4 / 6

Outreach that does not feel like spam.

  • Connect with a message that references something specific from their profile
  • Wait 3 days before sending a follow-up. Do not pitch in the first message
  • Lead with value first: share a useful insight for their industry
  • Propose a 15-minute call, not a formal proposal
