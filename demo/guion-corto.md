## The order

1. **The whole process** (0:00–0:26). Someone arrives from the firm's blog post,
   a pop-up invites them to ask, they ask, and it reaches the firm complete.
2. **And when they don't finish** (0:26–0:41). The drop-off comes only now, when
   the viewer already knows what there is to drop out of.
3. **Where the form lives** (0:41–0:50). The pop-up and the clip.
4. **Where it came from** (0:50–1:00). The blog post that brought it, and the
   campaign that paid for it.

**Line 3 is new and it matters:** the pop-up is a notice that invites people to
get in touch — it is not the form. The cut now shows it that way.
